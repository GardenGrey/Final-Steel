# Final Steel: Factions

*Working draft. See `01-story.md` for the setting.*

All three companies want the same thing: Magmore. What sets them apart is how they fight for it and how they mine it.

## Quick Comparison

| | **Azorious** | **Rakdos** | **Simic** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | Flight and ranged | Melee and close combat | Trickery, stealth and tech |
| Home world | Skyreach | Cinder | Verdis |
| Strength | Range, speed, vision | Armor, damage, durability | Surprise, control, adaptability |
| Weakness | Fragile up close | Slow, easy to kite | Weak in a straight fight |
| Mining edge | Fast, flying harvesters | Heavy, tough harvesters | Hidden, hard-to-spot harvesters |
| Style | Careful, precise | Aggressive, loud | Patient, deceptive |

---

## Azorious: The Sky Concord

**Fantasy:** Disciplined aerial engineers who win from a distance and never let a fight come to them.

**Combat:** Flying mechs and long-range weapons. Their units are fast and hit hard from afar, but a mech caught on the ground in melee dies quickly.

- **Units:** Skirmish fliers, artillery platforms, spotter drones
- **Tools:** Long-range sensors, beam weapons, air mobility
- **Strength:** Control of the sky and the battlefield's edges
- **Weakness:** Low armor, and big losses if pinned down
- **Mining edge:** Flying harvesters cross terrain quickly and grab distant seams first, but carry less per trip and die fast to any anti-air.

**Diplomacy personality:** Formal and legalistic. Honors contracts to the letter and expects the same. Slow to forgive a broken one.

## Rakdos: The Cinder Legion

**Fantasy:** Furnace-driven brawlers who treat every fight as a shoving match they intend to win.

**Combat:** Heavily armored mechs built to close the gap. Strong in short, brutal engagements.

- **Units:** Armored brawlers, siege rams, shock infantry
- **Tools:** Heat weapons, reinforced frames, charge abilities
- **Strength:** Raw durability and damage once in contact
- **Weakness:** Slow, little answer to air units or kiting
- **Mining edge:** Heavy harvesters haul the most ore and survive hits, but are slow and expensive.

**Diplomacy personality:** Blunt and proud. Respects strength, distrusts talkers. Easy to anger, but also quick to forgive a fair fight.

## Simic: The Verdis Collective

**Fantasy:** Bio-engineers and infiltrators who would rather you never see the trap.

**Combat:** Stealth mechs, adaptive systems, ambushes and disruption. Weakest in a stand-up fight, and deadliest if they pick the fight.

- **Units:** Cloaked skirmishers, repair support, hacking and disruption units
- **Tools:** Stealth fields, sabotage, self-repair
- **Strength:** Ambush, intel, and turning enemy tech against them
- **Weakness:** Fragile when revealed, and needs setup time
- **Mining edge:** Cloaked harvesters mine seams without being seen, and stealth lets them keep quiet claims. They are easy to kill once spotted.

**Diplomacy personality:** Friendly on the surface, with an agenda underneath. Offers deals that cost you later. Will happily sell you out if it pays.

## The Triangle

The three identities answer each other, which keeps matchups interesting without a strict rock-paper-scissors.

- **Azorious beats Rakdos** by staying out of reach and shooting.
- **Rakdos beats Simic** by smashing what's revealed. Stealth means little against area damage.
- **Simic beats Azorious** by surprising fliers and disrupting sensors and targeting.

None of these is a guaranteed win. Terrain, tier and Hollow interference all shift the odds.

## Contested Seams

With one resource, the map does the work. The three companies don't need each other's goods, but they do need the same ground.

- **Seams pull factions into conflict.** Two harvesters at one seam means a fight, and a fight means escorts.
- **Mining raises Disturbance.** Every faction's harvesters feed the Hollow, so the loudest miner draws the most attacks, and everyone suffers from the others' greed.
- **The Hollow forces the question.** When it arrives mid-fight, each faction chooses between a free-for-all and a truce, and its personality shapes how it answers.

| Faction | Likely response when the Hollow arrives |
|---|---|
| Azorious | Honors an existing truce, and offers a clean treaty if none exists |
| Rakdos | Keeps fighting until the Hollow hits them directly, then respects anyone who stands their ground |
| Simic | Accepts a truce, then quietly mines the abandoned seams |

## Open Questions

- Should each company have one signature super-unit at the top tier?
- Do we want a fourth minor faction (mercenaries) later?
- How strong should the mining edges be? They add flavor but could unbalance early game.
