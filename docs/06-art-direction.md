# Final Steel: Art Direction

*Working draft. Hex values are starting points for the palette, not final.*

## The Rule

**Every faction has a color, and neon green belongs to the Hollow alone.** A player should be able to tell who owns a unit, a structure or a shot at a glance, and green always means the Hollow.

## Faction Colors

| Faction | Colors | Dominant | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Azorious** | Blue and white | Blue, with white trim | Clean, bright and aerodynamic |
| **Rakdos** | Red and gold | **Red** | Red is the dominant color. Gold is the accent: trim, edges and details |
| **Simic** | Purple | Purple | Darker, with cloak shimmer effects |
| **The Hollow** | Sage gray | Sage gray | Very alien. Neon green glow |

## Faction Look and Feel

### Azorious
Light frames, slim limbs, wings and thruster vents. Smooth, polished panels. Blue armor with white trim and markings. Sleek, with long barrels on its rifles.

### Rakdos
Thick, blocky plating and visible rivets, furnace vents and scorched metal. **Mostly red**, with gold on edges, blades, insignias and trim. The axes and swords have gold edges. The howitzer is large and obviously heavy.

### Simic
Angular, lizard-like shapes, with gripping toes and fingers for climbing. Purple plating with a slightly organic, scale-like finish. When cloaked, the unit shimmers and bends light, like a heat haze with a purple tint.

### The Hollow
**Very alien.** The Hollow should look like nothing the corporations would build.

- **Sage gray** body, matte and slightly mottled, like stone or dried bone.
- Asymmetrical, with no straight lines. Ridges, plates and spines that look grown instead of built.
- **Neon green** glowing seams, eyes and cores. It reads as bioluminescent.
- Movement is uncanny: skittering, jerking, or too smooth.
- Hollow structures and burrows should look like growths in the ground, not buildings.

## Weapons and Effects

### Human Factions: Man-Made Weapons

Azorious, Rakdos and Simic attacks should look like **gunfire and typical man-made weapons**, so they feel grounded and industrial.

| Effect | Look |
|---|---|
| **Rifle fire** | Muzzle flashes and fast tracer rounds, warm yellow-orange |
| **Heavy shots** | Bigger muzzle flash, smoke, and a shock ring |
| **Howitzer** | A heavy thump and a plume of smoke. The shell arcs and lands with a big fiery explosion |
| **Melee hits** | Metal-on-metal sparks, with dents and scraping |
| **Explosions** | Orange and red fireballs, smoke and debris |
| **Simic shock hand** | Crackling electric arcs, blue-white with a purple tint. Not green |

Faction colors show on the **units and structures**, not on the shots. A Rakdos axe doesn't leave a red trail, and a rifle round fires like a rifle round.

### The Hollow: Neon Green

All Hollow weapons and energy attacks glow **neon green**.

| Effect | Look |
|---|---|
| **Resonance Cannon** | A thick neon green energy blast with a bright core and a rippling shockwave |
| **Pulse Lance** | A sharp neon green beam |
| **Rend Claw** | Green-glowing edges that leave a faint streak |
| **Adaptation** | Green ripples across the armor when it adapts to damage |
| **Disturbance** | A faint green haze and pulses in the ground over areas that are being heavily mined |

This keeps Hollow attacks easy to spot in a crowded fight, and helps players notice when the Hollow arrives.

## Other Visual Notes

- **Magmore** glows molten orange. It must stay distinct from Rakdos red and gold, so Magmore gets a brighter, more orange-yellow glow and lives in the ground and in bots, not on armor.
- **Wreckage** should be dimmed, with sparks, so it's visible but obviously dead. Hollow wreckage keeps a faint green glow, which is how players spot the rare, valuable pieces.
- **Cloaked** units are nearly invisible, with a faint purple shimmer at the edges.
- **Accessibility:** color should never be the only signal. Each faction also gets a distinct silhouette and an icon, so colorblind players can still read the battlefield.
