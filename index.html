Controls

  • Left-click select a unit or building. Drag to box-select units.
  • Right-click a smart order: ground = move, Magmore seam = mine, wreck = salvage, enemy = attack, friendly unit = protect.
  • M / A / D / E / P / R / S Move, Attack, Defend, Protect, Patrol, Retreat, Stop. Then click the target.
  • 1 / 2 / 3 stances: Aggressive, Defensive, Hold.
  • Select a building to build units. Right-click the ground to set where they go.
  • Arrow keys or screen edges pan the camera. Mouse wheel zooms. B jumps to your base. WASD pans when nothing is selected.
  • Space pauses. Orders are not allowed while paused.
